A About the project

Contributing

Feature suggestions, bug reports, support requests and offers to collaborate are all welcome. Open an issue on the GitHub repository or write to iuri@ufscar.br.

Citing the data

The land use and land cover maps come from the MapBiomas Project, Collection 10 of the Annual Series of Land Cover and Land Use Maps of Brazil (Souza et al., 2020), available at https://mapbiomas.org under a CC BY-SA licence. The sources of the external layers are listed in Chapter 3.
